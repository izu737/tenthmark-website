Questions

A few things
people ask us

How do you find and verify leads? +

We use Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Clay, and other professional tools to source and verify leads matched to your exact ICP. Every contact is verified before we start outreach — we do not work with unverified or recycled lists.

How long until I see results? +

Most clients see their first booked calls within 14 days of starting. Week one is onboarding and list-building. Outreach goes live in week two. The pipeline compounds from there as we optimize.

What industries do you work with? +

We work with marketing agencies, ad agencies, consulting firms, coaching businesses, recruiting agencies, PR agencies, B2B SaaS companies, and other professional service businesses. If you sell a high-ticket service to other businesses, we can likely help.

How does billing work? +

We charge a flat fee per qualified call booked on your calendar. No retainers, no monthly minimums. Pricing is discussed on your strategy call since it varies by industry, call volume, and campaign scope.

What do I need to provide? +

Very little. We need about 60 minutes for onboarding to understand your ICP, offer, and tone. After that, we handle everything. You'll review outreach copy before it goes live, and then you just manage your calendar.
